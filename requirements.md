Project Description

Your team has been hired to develop a simple classroom monitoring system for a university facilities department. 

The department wants to monitor classroom temperatures and identify when conditions fall outside an acceptable range.

Your task is to develop a Raspberry Pi-based application that processes temperature readings, determines whether the classroom environment is acceptable, and provides a visible warning when a temperature is outside the specified range. 

Project Requirements
-
- Allow the user to enter classroom temperature readings in Fahrenheit
- Determine whether a temperature is within the acceptable range of 68 and 76 degrees Fahrenheit, inclusive.
- Display an appropriate NORMAL or WARNING status.
- Calculate the average of the recorded temperatures.
- Save temperature readings and their statuses to a file.
- Use an LED connected to the Raspberry Pi to indicate a warning condition.
- Allow the acceptable temperature range to be changed according to a new client requirement.
